    About FLUENCY

    A friendly, safe speaking-practice companion

    Our Purpose

    FLUENCY was created for people who stammer, fumble, struggle with conversational flow, or lack confidence when speaking in front of others. Traditional clinical tools often feel intimidating, like a medical diagnosis dashboard. FLUENCY reimagines speech practice as a warm, daily ritual with a friendly animal companion who never judges your pauses.

    How the 4 Practice Modes Work

    🫁 Breath & Relax

    Diaphragmatic box breathing to reset vocal tension and anchor calm speech onset.

    📖 Reading Mode

    Paced teleprompter reading to measure words-per-minute, pauses, and smooth phonation.

    🎬 Image & Video

    Unscripted visual description practice to overcome impromptu speaking hesitation.

    💬 AI Conversation

    Interactive 1-on-1 voice dialogue partner simulating real-world cafés, interviews, and standups.

    Privacy & Transparency

    Your practice recordings and progress are stored privately and securely. We never expose your speech data to other users. Speech analysis is transparent, providing honest acoustic feedback without fake praise or medical claims.
